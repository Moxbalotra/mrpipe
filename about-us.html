MR PIPE is focused on creating reliable product experiences and stronger long-term relationships across the plumbing and distribution ecosystem. The MR PIPE Rewards platform is designed to make participation, points and rewards easier to manage.

01 Who We Are

MR PIPE is a brand serving the piping and plumbing ecosystem with a focus on dependable products, channel relationships and customer-oriented services. Alongside its business operations, MR PIPE provides a digital Rewards platform to support eligible dealers, retailers and plumbers.

The MR PIPE Rewards experience brings account management, points, eligible transfers and reward redemption into one digital platform.

02 Our Rewards Platform

MR PIPE Rewards is designed to give eligible participants a convenient way to manage their rewards activity from a mobile device.

  • Digital rewards account
  • Points balance and activity
  • Eligible point transfer features
  • Reward redemption
  • Support for account and transaction queries
