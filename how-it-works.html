Built for the MR PIPE partner network

The programme supports different partner roles, with permissions and point movement governed by the applicable programme rules.

PARTNER 01

Dealer

Dealers can participate in earning and, where the programme enables it, transfer eligible points to permitted downstream partners.

  • Earn eligible points
  • Transfer to Retailer or Plumber where enabled
  • Use points according to programme rules
PARTNER 02

Retailer

Retailers can receive and use eligible points, and can transfer permitted points onward to Plumbers where enabled.

  • Receive eligible points
  • Transfer to Plumber where enabled
  • Redeem according to available options
PARTNER 03

Plumber

Plumbers can receive eligible points and use them through the available redemption journey.

  • Receive eligible points
  • View points balance
  • Redeem available rewards

Point transfer flow

Important transfer rule

Points cannot be freely traded in every direction. The app controls the routes that are available to each participant.

Retailer:must not sell or transfer points back to a Dealer or higher-level participant where that route is not enabled.
Dealer:must not transfer points to a route that is not provided by the programme.
Everyone:unauthorised trading, manipulation or misuse of points may lead to action under the Rewards Policy.
