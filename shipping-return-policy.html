Shipping & Return Policy

This policy explains delivery, shipping, returns and replacements for physical products or orders offered by MR PIPE or through an authorized MR PIPE service.

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

01 Scope

This policy applies only where MR PIPE or an authorized channel offers physical products for purchase or delivery. If a particular product, invoice, order page or authorized seller provides different terms, those specific terms may apply to that transaction.

Important: MR PIPE Rewards points are not physical goods and are therefore not covered by shipping or product-return provisions unless expressly stated for a particular reward.

02 Order Processing

Orders are normally processed after successful payment confirmation and verification of the required order details.

  • Processing time may vary by product and location.
  • Orders may be delayed because of stock availability, verification, holidays or circumstances outside reasonable control.
  • An order may be cancelled if the product is unavailable or the transaction cannot be successfully verified.

03 Shipping & Delivery

Delivery timelines depend on product availability, destination, courier service and other operational factors. Any estimated delivery date is an estimate and not a guaranteed delivery deadline unless expressly stated otherwise.

Customers should provide a complete and accurate delivery address and contact number.

04 Delivery Issues

If a shipment is delayed, damaged, incorrectly delivered or appears to be missing, contact MR PIPE support as soon as reasonably possible with the order/reference number and relevant details.

MR PIPE may coordinate with the delivery partner to investigate the issue and determine the appropriate resolution.

05 Return Eligibility

Returns are accepted only for products or orders that are expressly eligible for return under the applicable order terms.

  • Products may need to be unused and in their original condition.
  • Original packaging, accessories and supporting documents may be required.
  • Products damaged after delivery through misuse, improper handling or unauthorized modification may not qualify.

06 Damaged or Defective Products

If a product arrives damaged or is found to have a verified manufacturing defect, contact support promptly. MR PIPE may request photographs, videos, order information or other reasonable evidence before approving a replacement, repair or refund.

Resolution may depend on the product's warranty and applicable seller or manufacturer terms.

07 Non-Returnable Situations

Unless required by applicable law, returns may not be accepted for products that have been used, altered, damaged through misuse, returned without required components, or otherwise fail the applicable return conditions.

Digital services, redeemed rewards and promotional benefits may also be non-returnable unless specifically stated otherwise.

08 Refunds for Approved Returns

Where a return is approved and a refund is applicable, the refund will normally be processed after the returned product is received and the applicable verification is completed.

The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment gateway, bank or payment service provider.

09 Cancellation Before Dispatch

Customers may request cancellation before an eligible order has been dispatched. Cancellation is not guaranteed once processing, dispatch or fulfilment has begun.

If cancellation is approved, any applicable refund will be handled according to the transaction's refund terms.

10 Exchanges & Replacements

Where an exchange or replacement is offered, availability depends on stock, product condition and the applicable transaction terms. MR PIPE may provide a replacement, repair, refund or another appropriate resolution for a verified eligible issue.

11 Contact for Returns & Delivery Issues

For shipping, return, replacement or delivery support, contact MR PIPE with your registered mobile number, order/reference ID, transaction date and a short description of the issue. Never share your password or OTP.

MR PIPE may update this policy when services, products, delivery arrangements or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
