Grievance & Data Request

Use this page to raise a grievance or request help regarding your MR PIPE Rewards account, personal information, privacy, data correction, deletion or other service-related concerns.

MR PIPE Customer Support

01 When to Use This Page

You may contact MR PIPE when you have a concern about your account, personal data, privacy, rewards activity, a transaction, support experience or another issue that requires review.

Privacy ConcernQuestions about how personal information is handled.
Data RequestRequest access, correction or eligible deletion.
GrievanceRaise a complaint or unresolved service issue.

02 Data Requests

Subject to applicable law and verification requirements, you may request assistance with:

  • Access to eligible personal information
  • Correction of inaccurate account information
  • Deletion of eligible personal information
  • Information about how your data is used
  • Questions about data retention or account closure

03 Grievances

You can raise a grievance about issues such as:

  • Account or profile problems
  • Incorrect points or reward activity
  • Privacy or data-handling concerns
  • Transaction or support-related issues
  • Technical problems that remain unresolved

04 How to Submit a Request

Send your request to the MR PIPE support team using the contact details below. Clearly mention that your message is a Grievance or Data Request and describe what you want reviewed or corrected.

Recommended details: registered mobile number, request type, relevant account/order/transaction reference, description of the issue, and supporting documents or screenshots where necessary.

05 Identity Verification

To protect user accounts and personal information, MR PIPE may ask for reasonable information to verify the identity or authority of the person making a request.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs or other confidential authentication credentials.

06 Request Review

MR PIPE will review requests based on the information provided, applicable program terms, technical records and legal requirements. Additional information may be requested when necessary to investigate or process a request.

If your request specifically concerns closing your MR PIPE Rewards account and deleting eligible personal data, use the dedicated account deletion page.
