03 Information to Include

To help us investigate your request faster, include:

  • Your registered mobile number
  • Order, transaction or reference ID, if applicable
  • Date and approximate time of the issue
  • A short description of the problem
  • Screenshots or supporting information when relevant

05 Support Safety

MR PIPE support will not require your account password, OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN or other confidential authentication credentials. Do not share such information with anyone claiming to provide support.

06 Response & Resolution

Support requests are reviewed according to their nature and available information. Resolution time may vary depending on the issue, transaction status, third-party service providers and verification requirements.
