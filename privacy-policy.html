Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how MR PIPE handles information when you use the MR PIPE Rewards mobile application and related services.

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

Important: This Privacy Policy should accurately reflect the actual data collected and processed by the MR PIPE Rewards application. Update the data categories below if your final app uses additional information or third-party services.

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the MR PIPE Rewards mobile application ("App") operated by MR PIPE ("MR PIPE", "we", "us", or "our").

It explains what information may be collected through the App, how that information may be used, how it may be protected, when it may be shared, and the choices available to users.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the App may collect information such as:

Information Possible Purpose
Name and profile information Account creation, identification and account management.
Mobile number Account authentication, OTP verification and support.
Email address Account communication, support and important service notifications where applicable.
Dealer / retailer / plumber information Account classification, rewards management and program administration.
Reward points and transaction information Calculating, maintaining and managing rewards, points and eligible transactions.
Device and technical information Security, diagnostics, performance and service improvement where applicable.

We only collect information that is necessary for the relevant features and purposes of the App, subject to the actual implementation of the application.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Authenticating users and protecting accounts.
  • Managing MR PIPE Rewards accounts and reward points.
  • Processing eligible reward transactions.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Sending important service-related notifications.
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse or unauthorized activity.
  • Maintaining and improving the App.
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements.

4. Reward Points and Transactions

If you participate in the MR PIPE Rewards program, information associated with your account may be used to maintain your reward balance and record eligible reward transactions.

Reward eligibility, earning, redemption, transfer, cancellation, expiry and other program rules may be governed by separate MR PIPE Rewards Terms and Conditions.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared with service providers or other parties only where reasonably necessary to operate the App, provide services, maintain infrastructure, process information, protect security, prevent fraud, or comply with applicable law.

Where third-party service providers process information on our behalf, we expect them to handle information according to applicable contractual and legal requirements.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services such as hosting, analytics, authentication, notification, crash reporting, payment or other infrastructure services, depending on the features enabled in the App.

Such services may process certain information necessary to provide their functionality. The actual third-party services used by the released App should be reflected in the final version of this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, to provide the requested services, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, enforce agreements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Where data is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymized in accordance with applicable requirements.

9. Account and Data Deletion

If the MR PIPE Rewards App allows users to create an account, users can request deletion of their account and associated data through the designated account deletion process.

Requests may be submitted through our dedicated account deletion page:

https://mrpipe.in/delete-account

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us using the details provided below.
